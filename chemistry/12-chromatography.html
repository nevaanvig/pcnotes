Paper Chromatography

Structure 1.1

Run the plate and watch the spots separate. Rᶠ is a ratio, so it stays fixed as the solvent climbs — and changes completely when you change the solvent.

Spot migration modelled as a partition between the mobile solvent and the water held in the paper, with 'like dissolves like' setting the solubility in each. Rf values are therefore illustrative of the trend rather than literature values.
