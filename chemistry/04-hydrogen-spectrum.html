Pick an electron transition and see where the photon actually lands. Only the Balmer series falls in the visible, and this shows why.

Wavelengths from the Rydberg equation 1/λ = R_H(1/n₁² − 1/n₂²) with R_H = 1.097 × 10⁷ m⁻¹. Energy levels drawn to scale as Eₙ = −13.6/n² eV.
