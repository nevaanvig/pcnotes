Complex Ion Colour

Structure 3.1 · HL

Metal plus ligand sets the d-orbital splitting, the splitting sets the absorbed wavelength, and what you see is the complement.

Splitting estimated with the Jørgensen approximation Δ ≈ f(ligand) × g(metal) × 1000 cm⁻¹ — beyond the syllabus, but it reproduces the real numbers. Observed colour from the standard complementary-colour table.
